What we manufacture

A panel for every point in the power line.

Each unit is designed to your single-line diagram and load list, then built, wired and tested in-house. Ratings below are typical — tell us your load and we engineer to it.

MCC · PCC

Motor Control & Power Control Centres

Fixed, draw-out and compartmentalised panels for distributing power and controlling motor loads across a plant.

  • Bus ratingup to 6300 A
  • Fault levelup to 50 kA
  • SeparationForm 2 – 4b
APFC · Capacitor

APFC & Capacitor Panels

Automatic power-factor correction to cut your demand charges and keep PF near unity — stepped switching with detuned reactors.

  • Correction25 – 1000 kVAR
  • Stages6 / 8 / 12-step
  • ReactorsDetuned 7% / 14%
MCCB · DB

MCCB Distribution Boards

Main and sub-distribution boards with MCCB incomers and outgoers, metered and labelled to your feeder schedule.

  • Incomer63 – 1600 A
  • EnclosureIP42 / 54 / 65
  • MeteringMultifunction meter
AMF · Synchro

AMF & DG Synchronising Panels

Auto mains-failure and generator synchronising panels for standby power — seamless mains/DG transfer with full protection.

  • ControlPLC / genset relay
  • TransferAuto / manual
  • SetsSingle & parallel

Common questions

Before you send the enquiry.

Do you build to our drawing, or do you design it?+

Both. Send your single-line diagram and we build exactly to it. No drawing yet? Share your load list or connected load and we'll size the panel, prepare the GA drawing and quote it for your approval.

What's your typical delivery time?+

It depends on size and component availability, but a standard panel is usually ready in a few weeks from drawing approval. We commit to a firm date at order confirmation and keep you updated through the build.

Which brands of switchgear do you use?+

We build with branded switchgear as standard — primarily Schneider and L&T — and will match whatever make your specification or client calls for.

Do you handle installation and commissioning?+

Yes. Alongside manufacturing, we take up site installation and commissioning — charged separately from the panel — or we coordinate with your own site electrical team if you prefer.

Can you supply for ongoing projects or tenders?+

Yes. We regularly supply EPC contractors and builders on a project and repeat-order basis. Mention your volume in the enquiry and we'll set up the right commercial terms.
